Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 13, 2026

Mahuri Technologies LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates mobile applications and the website mahurisolutions.com. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our services.

1. Information Collection and Use

We develop mobile applications with a focus on privacy. Depending on the specific application, we may collect minimal data required for functional performance:

  • Log Data: When you encounter an error or crash in our applications, anonymized crash logs may be collected automatically via standard platform tools, such as Apple Crashlytics or Google Play Console Vitals, to help us debug software issues.
  • Voluntary Information: If you contact us via email at info@mahurisolutions.com, we collect your email address and any text you provide solely to answer your support inquiry.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may use third-party infrastructure providers, such as standard Apple App Store and Google Play analytics or cloud hosting services, solely to monitor app health and push updates.

3. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We implement standard, industry-accepted development practices to secure data transferred via our mobile applications. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Children's Privacy

Our mobile applications do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and learn that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate steps to remove it from our systems.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
