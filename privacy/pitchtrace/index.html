PitchTrace Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Overview

Mahuri Solutions builds PitchTrace to help users record and review pitching sessions. This policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how to contact us with privacy questions.

Information we collect

PitchTrace may collect information you provide in the app, such as player profiles, session entries, pitch notes, and support messages. If analytics or crash reporting are enabled, the app may collect basic device, usage, and diagnostic information to improve reliability.

How we use information

  • To provide and maintain PitchTrace features.
  • To save user-entered training and session information.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To improve app performance, stability, and usability.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers for hosting, analytics, crash reporting, customer support, or app operations. These providers are used only to operate and improve PitchTrace.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Users may contact us to request deletion of information associated with their account, where applicable.

Children's privacy

PitchTrace is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where required.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Mahuri Solutions through the contact form.
