WorldLens

Local time, weather, and currency for anywhere in the world.

WorldLens turns a tap on the map into everything you need to know about a place: local time and time difference, current weather, elevation, and live currency exchange rates.

App Store Google Play Support

Features

Built for a quick check-in on anywhere.

Explore any place

Tap a point on the map to resolve its name, local time and time difference, weather, elevation, and currency exchange rate.

Save and organize

Save the places you care about and drag-and-drop to reorder them, on Android and iOS alike.

Home-screen widgets

See your saved places' time and weather right on your home screen, with a tap that jumps straight into that place -- on both Android and iOS.
