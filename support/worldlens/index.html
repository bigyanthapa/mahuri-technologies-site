Find quick answers or contact Mahuri Solutions for help with WorldLens.

How do I save a place?

Tap anywhere on the map to see its details, then use the save button on the location card. It will appear in the Saved tab.

Can I reorder my saved places?

Yes. On the Saved tab, press and drag a place's handle icon to reorder the list. The order also controls what the home-screen widget shows.

How do I add the home-screen widget?

On Android, long-press the home screen, choose Widgets, and add WorldLens. On iOS, long-press the home screen, tap the + button, and search for WorldLens.

Why does a saved place need location access?

WorldLens only uses location to center the map on where you are when you open it; saved places and their weather/time/currency data work without it too.
