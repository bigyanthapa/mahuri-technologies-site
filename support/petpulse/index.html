PetPulse Support

Find quick answers or contact Mahuri Solutions for help with PetPulse.

How do I add a pet?

Open PetPulse, go to the pet profile area, and choose the add option. Enter the pet's basic details and save the profile.

Can I create recurring reminders?

Yes. Use reminders for recurring care tasks such as feeding, medication, grooming, and appointments.

How do I report an issue?

Use the contact form below and include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of what happened.

Where is the privacy policy?

Read the PetPulse privacy policy.

Contact PetPulse Support

For support, email Mahuri Solutions and include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of the issue.
